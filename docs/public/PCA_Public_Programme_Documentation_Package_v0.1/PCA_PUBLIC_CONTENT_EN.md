# 5. Privacy `/privacy`

**SEO title:** PCA Privacy — Protection Without a Readable Central Child Profile  
**SEO description:** Understand PCA's local-first privacy approach, family-side data, minimum central technical records and claims that still require runtime proof.

## Hero
### Your child's activity belongs to you, not to us

PCA is designed to provide protection without building a readable central profile of your child's sensitive activity.

## What PCA processes locally

Some protection functions need information on the child device. For example, applying an app limit or schedule requires the device to understand relevant activity. PCA's approach is to process that information locally wherever possible.

## What may synchronize between trusted family devices

When sensitive protection information needs to move between trusted parent and child endpoints, PCA is designed to use end-to-end encrypted delivery where required. Production scope remains subject to security verification.

## What PCA central services need

Central services may keep minimum technical records for parent accounts/authentication, opaque device/child identifiers, enrollment, entitlement/licensing, encrypted delivery, delivery state, timestamps and minimum operational/security metadata.

## What PCA must not store centrally as readable child content

- photos and videos;
- arbitrary files/documents;
- messages;
- readable app-usage history;
- readable browsing history;
- readable precise-location history;
- microphone recordings;
- screenshots/background screen recordings;
- passwords or credentials.

## Why we do not say “zero data”

An online account service needs some technical and account information to operate. Saying “zero data” would hide that reality. The meaningful privacy promise is that PCA must not turn sensitive child activity into a readable central database.

# 9. FAQ `/faq`

**SEO title:** PCA Help & FAQ — Common Questions for Parents  
**SEO description:** Answers about PCA privacy, Parent PWA, Android/iOS status, child data, protection features and support.

## Does PCA collect my child's data?
PCA needs to process some information to provide protection. The design goal is to keep sensitive activity on trusted family-side devices or synchronize it end-to-end encrypted where required, instead of creating a readable central PCA profile. Central services still need minimum account and technical data to operate.

## Does PCA read my child's messages?
No routine PCA protection feature is designed to capture or centrally read personal messages.

## Does PCA collect photos, videos or files?
PCA does not need routine access to a child's photo library, videos or arbitrary files. These must not be collected into a readable central PCA database.

## Does PCA store browsing history centrally?
PCA's privacy rule is that readable browsing history must not be stored centrally. Web-safety functions may still need local processing to make protection decisions.

## Does PCA store app-usage history centrally?
Readable app-usage history must not become centrally readable PCA data. App use may be processed locally where needed for protection.

## Does PCA use location?
Only an approved location feature should use location, and it must be parent-controlled. Readable precise-location history must not become central PCA data. Actual feature availability is still runtime-gated.

## Is PCA Child available on Android?
Android is the primary PCA Child V1 platform direction. Final public download/store availability must be verified before links or badges are shown.

## Is PCA Child available on iPhone/iPad?
Not yet. The iOS child application is planned for a later release after its technical and platform requirements are complete.

## Can I use PCA Parent on iPhone/iPad?
PCA Parent is a responsive web experience. It is designed to work in supported mobile browsers, and install guidance may be available depending on the browser/platform. This is separate from the future iOS Child application.

## Do I have to install PCA Parent?
No. Installation is optional. You can continue using Parent Web in the browser.

## Does installing PCA Parent make my device trusted?
No. PWA installation is for app-like convenience. Trusted Browser authorization is a separate security/decryption function.

## Does PCA use AI?
Do not assume a production AI feature is active. PCA will describe AI publicly only after the specific capability is verified, privacy/security reviewed and approved for release.

## Can I delete my information?
PCA intends to give parents meaningful account and information controls, but exact Delete Now and retention behavior must be runtime-verified before a strong deletion promise is published.

## How do I report a problem?
Use Help & Feedback inside PCA Parent when available, or Contact for public support. Please do not include private information about your child.

## How much does PCA cost?
Final plans and prices are not yet approved for this public draft. PCA is being designed with affordability and broad access in mind.

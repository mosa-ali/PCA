# 01 — Product Vision, Scope and Principles

## 1. Vision

PCA helps parents and guardians set healthy digital boundaries for children while minimizing centralized collection of child data.

## 2. Product promise

- Parents control family policies.
- Child-device restrictions are transparent rather than hidden.
- Monitoring history is retained under family control, not as a readable PCA-company database.
- Arabic and English receive equal product quality.
- Emergency access remains available.
- Platform limitations are disclosed rather than disguised.

## 3. In scope

### Parent functions
- enroll and remove child devices;
- configure screen-time and continuous-use rules;
- configure app/category restrictions where platform allows;
- configure web/content filtering;
- view permitted activity summaries;
- view location/last-seen information;
- configure prayer reminders;
- approve temporary extra time;
- manage family parent roles;
- configure alerts/email destination;
- select data-retention policy;
- delete history immediately;
- initiate authorized recovery/removal.

### Child functions
- see remaining time and current rules;
- receive break/eye-distance/prayer reminders;
- request extra time;
- understand what is being monitored;
- use emergency functions;
- see protection status without secret surveillance behavior.

## 4. Out of scope

PCA will not:

- read private message contents by bypassing application security;
- record microphone conversations;
- secretly record camera footage;
- defeat OS sandboxing or root/jailbreak a device;
- decrypt HTTPS traffic by installing covert interception certificates;
- market spouse/partner tracking;
- use family activity for ads or commercial profiling;
- claim exact iOS/Android data that public APIs do not provide;
- classify a person's sexual orientation as a harmful-content category. Explicit sexual/adult material is filtered based on content, independent of identity or orientation.

## 5. Product modes

### Android Standard Mode
Normal consumer installation. Provides the strongest controls available without fully managed/device-owner provisioning. Hard non-bypassable enforcement is not promised where Android does not grant it.

### Android Protected Mode
Managed/device-owner style provisioning where legitimately supported and distributable. Provides stronger package, lock-task and anti-uninstall controls. This mode requires explicit compatibility and store/distribution validation.

### iOS Family Controls Mode
Uses Apple Family Controls, Managed Settings and Device Activity with parent/guardian authorization and required distribution entitlement. Uses Apple's privacy-preserving tokens and shields rather than unrestricted surveillance.

## 6. Commercial principle

Preferred model: family subscription or license. No behaviorally targeted advertising and no sale of child/family monitoring data.
